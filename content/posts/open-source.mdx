<p>
  <u>We love open source.</u>
</p>

Open source matters to us because most of the software we rely on exists because people chose to build in public and share the work.

<p>
  <u>We build with open source.</u>
</p>

Our platform depends on open source at every layer, from the frameworks in the frontend and backend to the tools that handle deployment, automation, and day-to-day development.

<p>
  <u>We give back to the open source community.</u>
</p>

We try to support open source in practical ways: contributing code, opening issues, sponsoring projects, and publishing our own tools when they are ready. Our CLI, libraries, and documentation are open source and available for anyone to use or improve.
