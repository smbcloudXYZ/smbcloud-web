Introducing smbCloud

[smbCloud](https://smbcloud.xyz) brings the infrastructure we use at [Splitfire AI](https://splitfire.ai) to smaller teams that do not want to run servers themselves.

With smbCloud, you can use compute, storage, databases, analytics, and other core infrastructure without buying hardware or guessing capacity months in advance.

You pay for what you use. That makes it easier to start small, test ideas, and scale when traffic or workload grows.

We built smbCloud so teams can spend less time maintaining servers and more time shipping. If you are running a website, building a new app, or need stronger storage and compute, smbCloud gives you the cloud basics without the usual overhead.

It runs on infrastructure already used inside [Splitfire AI](https://splitfire.ai), so reliability and security are part of the foundation, not an afterthought.
