Today we're shipping a full redesign of [smbcloud.xyz](https://smbcloud.xyz) and the [smbCloud Console](https://console.smbcloud.xyz).

## Why we redesigned

The old site was built to get something out the door quickly. It did the job, but it didn't reflect what smbCloud has grown into — a production cloud platform used by real teams deploying real apps every day.

We wanted a design that felt as precise and reliable as the infrastructure underneath it.

## What's new

### A unified design system

Both the marketing site and the console now share the same design tokens — colors, spacing, typography, and component patterns. Whether you're reading about smbCloud or deploying inside the console, it feels like the same product.

### Built for dark mode

The new site defaults to dark mode. It's easier on the eyes at 2am when you're debugging a deployment, which is exactly when you're likely to be here.

### A cleaner homepage

The new homepage cuts straight to what matters: what smbCloud does, how it works, and how to get started. The terminal demo now shows exactly what a `smb` deploy looks like across NodeJS, Swift, and Ruby — no prose needed.

### Improved navigation

Posts, KTI, and the Console link are all a single click from anywhere on the site. The header is sticky and stays out of your way.

### siGit

We also added a proper section for [siGit Code & Deploy](https://apps.apple.com/se/app/sigit-git-client-code-editor/id6753018849) — our native vibe coding app for iOS, Android, and Windows. It's `smbcloud-cli` reimagined as a beautiful, native experience. If you haven't tried it yet, you should.

## What didn't change

The platform itself. Deployments still work the same way. Your SSH keys, your projects, your apps — nothing moved.

## What's next

We're working on expanded documentation, a proper onboarding flow for new projects, and tighter integration between the console and the CLI. More soon.

— The smbCloud team
