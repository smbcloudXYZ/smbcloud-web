## Meet MoVibe

MoVibe is our mobile-first Git client, deployment app, and code editor from smbCloud. If you already use the CLI, this is the version built for quick work from your phone or tablet. It is designed for the moments when you need to review a change, make an edit, or trigger a deploy without opening a laptop.

### Why MoVibe?

- **Mobile-first**: Built for phones and tablets, so you can code, review changes, and deploy away from your desk.
- **Rust-based**: Fast, stable, and memory-safe, especially when you are working with larger codebases.
- **Vibecoding (WIP)**: Talk to your codebase in real time and work in an interface designed for touch and gesture.
